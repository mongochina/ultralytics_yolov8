工程地址：https://github.com/airockchip/ultralytics_yolo11

conda create -n ultralytics_yolo11 python=3.8

conda activate ultralytics_yolo11

pip install -U ultralytics

启动训练
yolo obb train data=ultralytics/data/ztl_multi_goods_rec_dataset_3.yaml model=yolo11s-obb.pt epochs=500 imgsz=640

在 PC 端推理测试验证
yolo obb predict model=runs/obb/train7/weights/best.pt source=test.jpg
